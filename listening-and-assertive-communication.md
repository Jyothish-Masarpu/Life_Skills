
# Listening and Active Communication

## 1. Active Listening

### Question 1: What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening?

1. Give full attention to the person who is speaking.
2. Don't interrupt them in the middle of a conversation.
3. Maintain eye contact and show that you are listening.
4. Avoid distractions like mobile phones and other thoughts.
5. Ask questions if you don't understand something.
6. Try to understand what the person is actually saying instead of just hearing their words.
7. Take notes if the conversation contains important information.
8. Repeat the important points in your own words to make sure you understood correctly.

## 2. Reflective Listening

### Question 2: According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening?

1. Listen carefully and let the other person finish speaking.
2. Try to understand their feelings along with their words.
3. Don't judge or immediately give your own opinions.
4. Repeat what you understood in your own words.
5. Ask questions if you are confused about something.
6. Pay attention to their facial expressions and body language.
7. Confirm with the speaker whether your understanding is correct.
8. Make sure both people have the same understanding of the conversation.

## 3. Reflection

### Question 3: What are the obstacles in your listening process?

I think my biggest problem is that sometimes I start thinking about what to reply while the other person is still speaking. Because of that, I may miss a few important points.

Sometimes I also get distracted by my thoughts, especially when I am thinking about other work. If someone explains something for a long time, maintaining the same concentration becomes difficult.

### Question 4: What can you do to improve your listening?

I can improve my listening by making a few changes in my daily routine.

1. I should listen completely before thinking about my reply.
2. If I don't understand something, I should ask instead of assuming.
3. During technical discussions, I should make a habit of taking notes.
4. I should not judge someone's opinion before hearing their complete point.
5. I should repeat important points to check whether I understood them correctly.
6. I should be patient, even when someone takes more time to explain something.

## 4. Types of Communication

### Question 5: When do you switch to Passive communication style in your day-to-day life?

Sometimes I become passive when I talk to seniors or people who have more experience than me. Even if I have a different opinion, I may not express it because I don't want to sound disrespectful. Sometimes I also agree with others even when I am not completely convinced, just to avoid unnecessary arguments.

### Question 6: When do you switch into Aggressive communication styles in your day-to-day life?

I sometimes become aggressive when I get frustrated, especially when I have explained something multiple times and the other person still doesn't understand my point. When things don't happen as expected, I may become impatient. Sometimes my tone becomes a little harsh without me realizing it. I think this usually happens when I am stressed or irritated.

### Question 7: When do you switch into Passive-Aggressive communication styles in your day-to-day life?

Sometimes when something hurts me, instead of directly telling the person what happened, I simply become silent. There are also situations where I use sarcasm or give short replies when I am annoyed. Sometimes I expect the other person to understand that I am upset without actually explaining it. Later, I realize that communicating directly would have been a better option.

## 5. Assertive Communication

### Question 8: How can you make your communication assertive?

I think being assertive means expressing what I feel and think without disrespecting the other person. To improve this, I can follow a few things:

1. I should express my opinions openly instead of keeping everything to myself.
2. I should learn to say no when I cannot do something rather than accepting everything.
3. If I disagree with someone, I should explain my reason calmly.
4. I should listen to the other person's opinion instead of trying to prove that I am always right.
5. If I have a problem with someone, I should talk to them directly instead of ignoring them.
6. I should maintain confidence while speaking and not feel nervous about sharing my thoughts.
7. I should accept my mistakes when I am wrong instead of blaming others.

I want to improve the way I communicate with people. I don't want to stay silent when I have something to say, and at the same time, I don't want to hurt someone while expressing my opinion. I think finding that balance is what makes communication assertive.

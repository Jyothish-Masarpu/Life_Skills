# Tiny Habits

### Question 1: In this video, what was the most interesting story or idea for you?

The most interesting idea for me was starting with very small habits, like doing one push-up. Small actions may look simple, but doing them regularly can create big changes over time.

### Question 2: How can you use B = MAP to make making new habits easier? What are M, A and P?

B = MAP means Behaviour = Motivation, Ability and Prompt.
- **M – Motivation:** Desire to do something.
- **A – Ability:** How easy it is to do.
- **P – Prompt:** A reminder to do it.

We can build habits by starting small, making them easy and connecting them to an existing routine.

### Question 3: Why is it important to "Shine" or Celebrate after each successful completion of habit?

Celebrating small achievements creates positive feelings and motivates us to repeat the habit. Even completing a small task deserves appreciation because it helps us stay consistent.

### Question 4: In this video, what was the most interesting story or idea for you?

The most interesting idea was improving by just 1% every day. Small improvements in our daily activities can lead to big results over time. Consistency matters more than immediate success.

### Question 5: What is the book's perspective about Identity?

The book explains that habits help build our identity. Instead of saying, "I want to learn coding," we should say, "I am a person who codes every day." Small actions build the identity we want.

### Question 6: Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit easier to do?

We can make habits easier by starting with small steps, preparing things in advance and reducing unnecessary effort. The Two-Minute Rule is a good example of this.

### Question 7: Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit harder to do?

We can make bad habits harder by removing triggers, adding extra steps and avoiding situations that encourage them. For example, keeping our phones away while working can reduce unnecessary usage.

I want to improve my daily coding practice. I will set a fixed time, start with one problem, keep my laptop ready and track my progress. I will also appreciate myself for completing the daily practice.

### Question 9: Pick one habit that you would like to eliminate or do less of? What are the steps that you can take to make the cue invisible or the process unattractive or hard or the response unsatisfying?

I want to reduce unnecessary mobile phone usage. I will turn off notifications, keep my phone away while working and set screen-time limits. This will help me focus more on useful activities.

### Question 1
**What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line.**

The Feynman Technique is a way of learning where we first identify what we don't understand and then try to explain it in simple words, just like teaching someone else.

### Question 3
**What are active and diffuse modes of thinking?**

- **Focused Mode:** It is when we concentrate on a problem and use logic and familiar concepts to understand it.
- **Diffuse Mode:** It is when our mind is relaxed and makes new connections, which helps us understand difficult topics and solve problems.

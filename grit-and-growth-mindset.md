**Q2. Paraphrase the video:**

A growth mindset means believing that we can improve our skills through learning and practice. We should accept mistakes as learning opportunities instead of being afraid of failure.

**Q3. What is Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?**

An internal locus of Control means believing that our actions and decisions affect our results. The key point is to take responsibility for our actions instead of blaming others or situations.

## Prevention of Sexual Harassment (PoSH)

### 1. What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

Sexual harassment includes any unwelcome behaviour of a sexual nature, whether verbal, non-verbal, physical or online. Examples include:

- Unwanted physical contact, touching, hugging or inappropriate gestures.
- Making sexual jokes, comments or inappropriate remarks about someone's appearance.
- Sending inappropriate messages, images or videos.
- Asking personal or intrusive questions about someone's private or sexual life.
- Repeated unwanted invitations or requests for sexual favours.
- Threatening someone or offering workplace benefits in exchange for sexual favours.

Such behaviour is unacceptable even if the person claims it was meant as a joke.

### 2. What would you do if you faced or witnessed such behaviour?

If I experience or witness sexual harassment, I would:

- Clearly communicate that the behaviour is inappropriate and must stop, if it is safe to do so.
- Support the affected person and listen without judging or blaming them.
- Record the incident, including dates, times, messages and any available evidence.
- Report the incident to HR or the Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) through the appropriate channel.
- Maintain confidentiality and cooperate with any investigation.
- Avoid confronting or spreading rumours about the people involved.
- Report repeated incidents immediately and never ignore such behaviour.

I believe everyone has a responsibility to maintain a safe, respectful and inclusive workplace.
